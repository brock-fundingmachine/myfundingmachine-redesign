## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Business-funding brokers, both established and aspiring, who are evaluating software to run their funding business. They are usually comparing Funding Machine against staying in a broker/ISO program where they hand deals to someone else's reps and keep 20–50% of the revenue. (Confirmed: brokers are the primary visitor; booking a demo is the primary action.)

## Product Purpose

Funding Machine is "The Funding Broker OS": lending-management software that gives a broker end-to-end control of each deal, from client application through AI credit analysis, funding plan, agreement, lender applications, approval and funding, and the broker's own invoice. Success on the marketing site means a qualified broker books a demo (`/book-demo`); watching the demo video is the secondary path.

## Positioning

It was built from the inside of a working funding company, which still operates today (CEO & Founder Leo Kanell). The broker keeps 100% of the revenue on their deals instead of 20–50% through a brokered program, keeps deal control, can build their own affiliate system, and can monetize their own data.

## Operating Context

Brokers work a pipeline of small-business clients: credit pulls, underwriting considerations, card stacking and term-loan funding plans, contracts for signature, calls and texts with clients, and invoices for their fee. The product lives at app.myfundingmachine.com. The company also runs a community, a Masterclass, and the yearly FM Live event (FM Live 2027, Feb 25–28, Orlando, FL).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Automated underwriting in seconds; instant AI credit analysis (risks, cautions, strengths).
- Contract generation in clicks; built-in call and text; invoice generation and sending.
- Pipeline and deal analytics; client management with search, filters and status tracking.
- Multi-source funding plans (card stacking, term loans, more).
- Step-by-step automated workflows from underwriting confirmation to funding completion.
- Community and education.
- Supports funding operations across all 50 states. Personal funding is supported but subject to separate licensing and compliance that is the broker's responsibility.
- The revenue calculator compares 100% revenue against a 30% brokered commission and must keep its earnings disclaimer.
- Testimonials must keep "Results vary based on individual effort and market conditions."

## Product Principles

1. Show the deal moving: the product's proof is the pipeline from application to funded to invoiced, not adjectives.
2. Ownership over commission: every claim ladders back to "keep 100% of the revenue and the control."
3. Built by operators: the credibility is that the founders still run a funding company.
4. Honest numbers: illustrative figures are labelled, and earnings language always keeps its disclaimer.
