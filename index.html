What is the Funding Machine?

The Funding Machine is an AI-powered lending management platform designed for brokers to manage their entire funding pipeline, from application to funding, with full control and automated underwriting.

How do I sign up for the Funding Machine?

You can sign up by booking a demo with our team. We’ll walk you through the platform and help you set up your account to start scaling your funding business.

Can Funding Machine owners provide financing options in all 50 states?

Yes, the platform is designed to support funding operations across all 50 states, allowing you to scale your business nationwide.

Can I offer personal funding with my Funding Machine?

Yes. Funding Machine supports both personal and business funding workflows, giving you flexibility in the products you offer. However, personal funding products are subject to different federal and state laws and regulations. It is your responsibility to ensure you are properly licensed and compliant in the jurisdictions where you operate. Certain products may also require the funds to be used for business purposes.

Do I need previous funding experience?

While previous experience is helpful, our platform and masterclass are designed to provide you with the tools and knowledge needed to succeed in the funding industry.

Do I have to have a legal entity to purchase a Funding Machine?

We recommend having a legal entity for your business operations, but you can discuss your specific situation with our team during the demo.
