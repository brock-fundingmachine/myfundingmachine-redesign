About Funding Machine

We’re revolutionizing the business lending industry with AI-powered tools and comprehensive training that helps entrepreneurs succeed.

Our story

Funding Machine was born from a simple observation: business lending was broken. Entrepreneurs and funding professionals were losing deals, time, and money to outdated processes and fragmented tools.

We set out to build something different—a comprehensive platform that combines cutting-edge AI technology with proven business strategies to help funding professionals take control of their business.

Today, Funding Machine powers hundreds of successful funding businesses across all 50 states, processing millions in deals every month.
