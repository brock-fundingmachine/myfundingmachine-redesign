# Design System: Funding Machine

This file documents the design system shipped in this repository (`index.html` and `assets/`).

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Category Standard, Built to Spec"**

Convention is the commitment. The page is a familiar B2B SaaS homepage in the live Funding Machine brand (blue, Inter, pill buttons), built at the fidelity of Linear, Stripe and Ramp. No irony and no quirk sneaks in; the difference from a template comes from craft alone. That means real product screens in a browser frame, real testimonial faces, one CTA label, AA contrast on every filled control, and an even section rhythm.

The ground is a near-white cool grey, and white bands alternate with it to separate sections. Surfaces are white cards defined by a hairline ring plus a soft, navy-tinted layered shadow. Blue does the work: the deeper blue fills every button and link so white text passes AA, and the lighter live blue is kept for marks and accents. The live cyan-violet-blue gradient survives only as a blurred halo behind the hero product frame and a glow inside the final CTA panel. Navy appears twice, as the event bar at the top and the CTA panel at the end, and the CTA panel is the page's one theme switch.

Motion is state feedback plus one authored moment: the How it works progress line fills, and each step dot lights in turn, as the stepper scrolls through view. There is also one short settle when the hero loads. Hover effects run only on fine pointers, presses scale down slightly, and reduced motion removes all of it.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-white cool ground, white bands and cards, navy ink; one deep action blue.
- Inter throughout at heavy display weights (800) with tight negative tracking.
- Pill buttons and chips; generous card radii (12 to 28px) that step up with container size.
- Hairline ring plus a layered navy-alpha shadow instead of visible borders on cards.
- Gradient as blurred background light only; never on text, never as a fill.
- One authored scroll moment (the stepper), one hero settle; everything else is state feedback.

## Colors

A cool blue-grey neutral field with one committed action blue, two light blue tints, and a three-stop halo gradient used only as background light.

### Primary
- **Action Blue** (`blue-600`): every filled button, every text link, the focus outline, the hero accent word "Smarter", check icons, step dots, the FM column's figures and the open FAQ toggle. It is deep enough that white text on it passes AA. That is why it, and not the live brand blue, carries every action.
- **Pressed Blue** (`blue-700`): hover state of the primary button and of arrow links; heading text of the Funding Machine comparison column.
- **Live Brand Blue** (`blue`): the brand's own primary. Used for marks only (the founder quote's quotation mark). Never under white text.

### Secondary
- **Blue Mist** (`blue-50`): the tint behind product screenshots in feature showcases, the Funding Machine column of the comparison table, and the light button's hover.
- **Blue Haze** (`blue-100`): the inset ring around Blue Mist panels and the row dividers inside the Funding Machine column.

### Tertiary
- **Halo Cyan** (`cyan`), **Halo Violet** (`violet`), **Halo Blue** (`brand-blue`): the three stops of the live brand gradient (90deg, cyan → violet at 50% → blue). They only appear as a blurred ellipse: behind the hero browser frame (blur 72px, opacity 0.32) and rising from the bottom of the CTA panel (blur 90px, opacity 0.55). Cyan also tints the arrow icon on the event bar's link.

### Neutral
- **Navy Ink** (`ink`): headings and primary text.
- **Slate** (`ink-2`): body copy, ledes, nav links, card descriptions.
- **Muted Slate** (`ink-3`): captions, notes, disclaimers, timestamps, the competitor column's text.
- **Faint Slate** (`ink-faint`) and **Off Mark** (`mark-off`): the competitor's big figures and the "no" icons in the comparison. Deliberately recessive.
- **Line Hover** (`line-hover`): ring/border colour on hover for secondary buttons and FAQ toggles.
- **Mist Line** (`line-2`): input borders, secondary button border, FAQ rules, the stepper's unfilled track, browser-frame dots.
- **Hairline** (`line`): section band borders, table rules, card rings, header rule when scrolled.
- **Cool Ground** (`bg`): page background, the calculator card and the ring that separates step dots from the track.
- **Raised Ground** (`bg-raised`): the browser frame's title bar.
- **Surface White** (`surface`): cards, bands, inputs, footer; also the text colour on blue and on navy.
- **Deep Navy** (`navy`): the event bar, the CTA panel and the video player's empty state. **On-Navy Muted** (`on-navy-muted`) is secondary text on navy; primary text on navy is Surface White.

### Translucent roles
- **Hairline Ring** (`hairline-ring`): the 1px ring that defines frames, players and screenshots.
- **Hover Tint** (`hover-tint`): the pill wash behind hovered nav links.
- **Scrim** (`scrim`): the frosted label on video players.
- **Glass** (`glass`): the sticky header background, under `saturate(1.6) blur(14px)`; 0.92 opacity in the mobile menu state.
- **Focus Halo** (`focus-halo`) and **Selection** (`selection`): Action Blue at low alpha for the input focus ring and text selection.

### Named Rules
**The Deep Blue Acts Rule.** Anything clickable that is filled or coloured uses Action Blue (`blue-600`) or Pressed Blue (`blue-700`). The lighter live blue never sits under white text.

**The Gradient Is Light Rule.** The cyan-violet-blue gradient is only ever a blurred background glow behind something else. It never fills text, buttons, borders, or a visible shape.

**The One Theme Switch Rule.** The final CTA panel is the only place the page turns dark (navy with white text). The event bar is chrome, not a section. No other section inverts.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter (same stack), self-hosted variable woff2, weights 400 to 800
**Label/Mono Font:** none; figures use Inter's tabular numerals

**Character:** One family carries everything. Headings are heavy (800) and tightly tracked so they read as product UI, not editorial. Body text runs at 400 with Inter's `cv11` and `ss03` alternates switched on page-wide.

- **Figure** (800, clamp 40px to 56px, 1, -0.04em, tabular): the calculator's annual totals. **Figure Compare** (800, 32px, 1.1, -0.03em; 26px on mobile): the big numbers in the comparison's first row.

- **Body Large** (17px, 1.6): showcase copy and FAQ questions (at 600, -0.01em). **Body** (16px, 1.6; 1.65 for FAQ answers). **Body Small** (15px, 1.6): card descriptions, footer, table cells.

### Named Rules
**The Tight Display Rule.** Tracking tightens as size grows: -0.04em on the hero and calculator figures, -0.03em on comparison figures, -0.025em on H2/H3, -0.015em on card titles, -0.01em on buttons and step titles. Body text is never tracked.

**The Tabular Money Rule.** Every number that changes or is compared (countdown, durations, calculator figures, step numbers) uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

**The No Kicker Rule.** Section heads are an H2 and an optional lede, centred, max 820px. Nothing sits above the H2: no eyebrow, no uppercase label, no pill tag.

## Layout

Content sits in a 1200px wrap with a fluid gutter (clamp 20px to 40px). Sections get the same vertical rhythm (clamp 80px to 128px top and bottom), and the space below a section head is constant (clamp 40px to 64px). Sections alternate between the Cool Ground and white "band" sections bordered by a hairline top and bottom (comparison, calculator, testimonials). That alternation, not dividers or colour blocks, is what separates them.

The hero is a centred stack: H1, lede, two buttons, trust row, checklist, then the wide browser frame. Its top edge shows above the fold at 1440x900. Feature showcases are asymmetric 5fr/7fr splits that flip on alternate rows; the feature cards sit on a six-column grid (three cards spanning 2, then two spanning 3); the calculator is a 5fr/7fr card; the FAQ is a 4fr/7fr split with a sticky head; testimonials are 11fr/6fr (stage and list). The awards strip bleeds to the viewport edge with scroll-snap, padded so it aligns to the wrap.

Breakpoints: 1080px tightens nav padding; 960px moves the nav into a drop panel and stacks every split to one column (the stepper turns vertical); 720px stacks the hero buttons to full width (max 360px), removes the browser frame's radius, and reflows the comparison table into labelled blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is lifted, softly. Cards never use a visible stroke as the whole edge; they combine a 1px hairline ring (as `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px`) with an ambient shadow tinted toward navy (oklch 0.2 0.04 265), never neutral black. There are three steps of that shadow, and the step tracks importance: inputs and resting cards get the small one, tables and players the medium, the hero frame and showcase screenshots the large, whose last layer is tinted blue. Black-alpha shadows appear only on elements that sit over photos or navy (player button, play badge, light button).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Small** (`0 1px 2px oklch(0.2 0.04 265 / 0.06)`): inputs, secondary button, resting feature cards.
- **Medium** (`0 1px 2px oklch(0.2 0.04 265 / 0.05), 0 6px 16px -4px oklch(0.2 0.04 265 / 0.08)`): table card, calculator card, players, hovered feature card.
- **Large** (`0 1px 2px oklch(0.2 0.04 265 / 0.04), 0 12px 32px -8px oklch(0.2 0.04 265 / 0.12), 0 40px 80px -24px oklch(0.45 0.2 263 / 0.22)`): the hero browser frame and showcase screenshots.
- **Primary glow** (`inset 0 1px 0 oklch(1 0 0 / 0.18), 0 1px 2px oklch(0.3 0.15 263 / 0.3), 0 6px 16px -6px oklch(0.546 0.215 262.9 / 0.55)`): the primary button only.

### Named Rules
**The Ring Plus Shadow Rule.** A card's edge is `0 0 0 1px` of Hairline or Hairline Ring layered under a navy-tinted shadow step. Do not swap in a solid border or a black drop shadow.

**The Hover Lifts Two Pixels Rule.** The only hover elevation is a feature card moving up 2px and stepping from the small shadow to the medium one, with its ring deepening to Mist Line.

## Shapes

The form language is soft and rounded. Every button, nav link, URL bar and player label is a full pill (999px). Containers round more as they get bigger: inputs and testimonial thumbnails at 12px, screenshots inside showcase panels at 14px, cards, browser frame and players at 16px, the comparison table at 20px, showcase media panels and the calculator at 24px, the CTA panel at 28px (20px on mobile). Brand mark tiles round at roughly a quarter of their size (8px on 32px, 6px on 24px, 5px on 20px), and the event-bar countdown chips use 6px. Avatars, step dots, play buttons, the FAQ toggle and social links are circles. The only straight-cornered surface is the browser frame at ≤720px, where it runs full-bleed.

### Named Rules
**The Radius Grows With Area Rule.** A nested shape always has a smaller radius than its container (24px panel → 14px screenshot; 16px frame → pill URL bar).

## Components

Confident, compact pills.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** fill every button and colour every link with Action Blue (`blue-600`), hovering to Pressed Blue (`blue-700`), so white text passes AA.
- **Do** use the single CTA label "Book a Demo" everywhere it appears (header, hero, mid-page, CTA panel); "Watch Demo" is the only secondary action.
- **Do** define cards with a 1px ring plus a navy-tinted shadow step (small, medium, large), never with a black shadow.
- **Do** keep buttons, nav links and labels as full pills (999px) and step container radii up with size (12, 16, 20, 24, 28px).
- **Do** track display type tight: -0.04em on the hero and calculator figures, -0.025em on H2 and H3.
- **Do** gate hover effects behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, give pressable things an `:active` scale (0.97 buttons, 0.95 play and toggle, 0.98 thumbnails), and use the ease-out curve `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for state changes.
- **Do** honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: no hero settle, no smooth scroll, a static complete stepper, near-zero transitions.
- **Do** show real product screens in the browser frame and real testimonial faces; keep the calculator's earnings disclaimer and the testimonial results line.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put the gradient on text, buttons, borders or any visible fill; it is only a blurred background glow behind the hero frame and in the CTA panel.
- **Don't** add eyebrow kickers, uppercase labels or pill tags above headings.
- **Don't** put icons in coloured tiles or circles as decoration; feature icons sit inline with their heading in Action Blue.
- **Don't** nest cards inside cards; a panel holds a screenshot or a table, not another card.
- **Don't** invert any section other than the final CTA panel; navy is reserved for the event bar and that one panel.
- **Don't** add a second CTA label or reword "Book a Demo".
- **Don't** add authored motion beyond the stepper fill and the hero settle; everything else is state feedback.
- **Don't** use the lighter live blue (`blue`) under white text.
