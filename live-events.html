Meet your speakers

Industry leaders and innovators sharing strategies to scale your funding business.

  • Leo Kanell

    Founder & CEO, 7 Figures Funding

    Co-Founder of the Funding Machine, host of the 7 Figures Club Podcast, and author of The Business Funding Formula. Over the past decade, Leo has helped tens of thousands of entrepreneurs secure hundreds of millions in funding.

  • Ty Talbot

    Chief Revenue Officer & Co-Founder, Funding Machine

    Ty Talbot is the Chief Revenue Officer and Co-Founder of Funding Machine, Co-Founder of the My Figures Cashflow Platform, and former COO of 7 Figures Funding. He is passionate about helping entrepreneurs access capital while equipping funding organizations with the technology, systems, and strategies they need to scale.

  • Nate Russell

    Co-Owner & Director of Funding, 7 Figures Funding

    With deep expertise across sales, underwriting, processing, and fulfillment, Nate has overseen tens of millions in approvals and built a reputation as a trusted leader in the funding industry.

  • Frankie DiAntonio

    Founder & CEO, Lexington Capital Holdings

    Frankie DiAntonio is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Lexington Capital Holdings, a commercial finance brokerage based in Port Jefferson Station, New York. He founded the company in 2022 at age 25 after earning his MBA in Finance from Stony Brook University. Under his leadership, Lexington Capital Holdings has grown to more than 85 employees, funds over $15 million in business financing each month, and was ranked No. 668 on the 2025 Inc. 5000 list of America’s Fastest-Growing Private Companies. DiAntonio is an Official Member of the Forbes Finance Council and the founder of The Lexington Foundation, a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting families in need throughout the Long Island community. He is recognized for building one of Long Island’s fastest-growing companies through a people-first culture, a commitment to his community, and a mission to expand access to capital for small and medium-sized businesses.

  • Katrina Fitten

    Founder & CEO, New Day For You Financial™

    Nationally recognized funding expert, author, Certified Master Financial Coach, and business strategist committed to helping CEOs turn access to capital into long-term success. As the founder and CEO of New Day For You Financial™, she created the Funding Power Platform™ to give leaders the tools and guidance they need to fund what’s next. Katrina also leads the She’s Fully Funded Club®, a growing network of business experts helping the next generation of women entrepreneurs become founded, funded, and flourishing.

  • Ryan Lind

    Founder, Zero Interest Mastery

    Ryan Lind began his journey to financial freedom by house hacking his first property, which sparked his passion for building passive income. After mastering 0% introductory rate business funding, he used it to launch multiple income streams, including e-commerce, Airbnb investments, and crypto. Having secured over $300,000 in 0% introductory rate funding himself, Ryan now helps entrepreneurs access strategic financing to grow without high-interest debt. Through Zero Interest Mastery, he and his team have helped more than 200 clients secure over $15 million in 0% introductory rate funding and counting.

  • Brock Jeppesen

    CTO, My Figures & Funding Machine

    Brock leads engineering for finance and data products, designing data pipelines, underwriting workflows, and partner integrations with an API-first approach to system design.

  • Kevin Hill

    Head of Technical Operations, My Funding Machine

    With over a decade of experience in the funding space, Kevin is a CRM expert obsessed with building systems and operations that eliminate unnecessary friction and create seamless experiences.

  • Robert McKinney

    Chief Operating Officer, Funding Machine

    Robert McKinney is the Chief Operating Officer of Funding Machine. He combines operational leadership, technology, and process optimization to help funding organizations scale while giving entrepreneurs the tools they need to make informed financial decisions. Robert is passionate about building systems that make funding simpler, more efficient, and more accessible.
